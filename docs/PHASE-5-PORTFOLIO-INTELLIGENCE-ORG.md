# Phase 5 — Portfolio Intelligence Team

## Executive owner

VID owns portfolio-level decisions. Portfolio Intelligence provides evidence-backed recommendations; it does not bypass Venture OS or CEO authority.

## Shared functional leaders

- PULSE — performance measurement and portfolio health
- ECHO — experiment design, outcome comparison, and organizational learning
- SCOUT — market/context signals when external evidence is required
- QUANTUM — observed revenue/cost reporting; no autonomous money movement

## Specialist responsibilities

- Metric Integrity: validates units, periods, non-negative values, and missing-data semantics.
- Attribution: groups observed outcomes by business and source without delimiter collisions.
- Forecasting: produces labeled projections from explicit assumptions; projections are never stored as realized outcomes.
- Experiment Manager: creates and transitions controlled tests through an explicit state machine.
- Portfolio Analyst: produces health and optimization recommendations from verified evidence.
- Decision Auditor: records recommendations and preserves evidence lineage for later CEO review.

## Authority boundary

Portfolio Intelligence may calculate and recommend. It does not execute external spending, payments, employment decisions, or irreversible commercial actions. Those actions remain governed by Venture OS and the Phase 1 delegated-authority contract.

## 95% autonomy model

Routine measurement, snapshotting, forecasting, experiment bookkeeping, attribution, and reporting are eligible for autonomous execution. Decisions with external financial or irreversible consequences remain subject to the existing authority level and CEO approval rules.
