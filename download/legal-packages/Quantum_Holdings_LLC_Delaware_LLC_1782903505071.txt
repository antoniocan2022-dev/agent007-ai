OPERATING AGREEMENT

OF

QUANTUM HOLDINGS LLC

This Operating Agreement (the "Agreement") is made and entered into as of ______ day of _______________, 20__, by and among Test Owner, with address [Owner Address] (the "Member").

ARTICLE I
FORMATION AND PURPOSE

1.1 Formation. The Member hereby forms a limited liability company under the name Quantum Holdings LLC (the "Company") under the Delaware Limited Liability Company Act (the "Act").

1.2 Purpose. The purpose of the Company is to engage in any lawful act or activity for which a limited liability company may be organized under the Act. The Company's business will primarily focus on investment activities.

1.3 Term. The Company shall have a perpetual existence, unless terminated earlier in accordance with this Agreement.

ARTICLE II
MEMBERSHIP

2.1 Initial Member. The initial Member of the Company is Test Owner.

2.2 Additional Members. The Company may admit additional Members in accordance with the provisions of this Agreement.

ARTICLE III
CAPITAL CONTRIBUTIONS

3.1 Initial Capital Contribution. The Member's initial capital contribution to the Company consists of [PLACEHOLDER: Description of Contribution, e.g., cash, property, services] valued at [PLACEHOLDER: Value of Contribution].

3.2 Additional Contributions. The Company may require additional capital contributions from Members as necessary for the business of the Company.

ARTICLE IV
ALLOCATION AND DISTRIBUTION

4.1 Allocations. Net profits and losses of the Company shall be allocated to the Member(s) in proportion to their respective capital contributions.

4.2 Distributions. Distributions of the net assets of the Company shall be made to the Member(s) in proportion to their respective capital contributions, unless otherwise agreed by the Member(s).

ARTICLE V
MANAGEMENT

5.1 Management. The Company shall be managed by the Member(s). The Member(s) shall have the authority to manage the business and affairs of the Company.

5.2 Authority. The Member(s) shall have the full authority to take any action necessary or appropriate for the conduct of the business of the Company, except as may be limited by law or this Agreement.

5.3 Meetings. The Member(s) may hold meetings at such times and places as they may determine. Meetings may be conducted in person or by means of communication equipment allowing all persons participating to hear each other.

ARTICLE VI
ADDITIONAL PROVISIONS

6.1 Governing Law. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware.

6.2 Amendments. This Agreement may be amended or modified only by the written consent of the Member(s).

6.3 Entire Agreement. This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement among the Member(s) and supersedes all prior agreements and understandings.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Member has executed this Operating Agreement as of the date first written above.
